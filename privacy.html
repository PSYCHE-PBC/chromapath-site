ChromaPath Privacy Policy

Last updated November 4, 2026. ChromaPath is made by Psyche Project PBC.

No account

ChromaPath has no accounts and does not ask for your name or email. Your level, hints and settings stay on your device.

Ads

The free game shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect your device's advertising identifier, your approximate location from your IP address, and how you interact with ads, and may use them to show and measure ads. On iPhone you choose whether to allow tracking, and in some regions you are asked for consent first. If you decline, ads are not personalised. Google's policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

Purchases go through Apple or Google. We use RevenueCat to confirm them, which receives an anonymous app ID and your purchase history in this app. We never see your payment details. RevenueCat's policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

Children

ChromaPath is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device. For anything else, email us.
